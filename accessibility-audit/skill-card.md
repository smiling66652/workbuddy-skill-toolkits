## Description: <br>
Generate a WCAG 2.2 accessibility audit checklist and remediation suggestions for UI, components, products, or design specifications. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Designers, developers, product teams, and accessibility reviewers use this skill to check a UI or design against WCAG 2.2 and produce a prioritized remediation checklist. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide UI screenshots or product descriptions to the agent for review. <br>
Mitigation: Review inputs for sensitive or confidential information before sharing them with the agent. <br>
Risk: Accessibility recommendations can be incomplete if the provided UI description or image does not include important states or assistive-technology requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Include platform, target WCAG level, relevant states, and known assistive-technology needs, then review the resulting checklist before using it for release decisions. <br>
